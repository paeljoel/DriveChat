DriveChat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

DriveChat is a messaging app that runs entirely in your web browser. It has no servers of its own: your messages are stored in your own Google Drive. This policy explains what data DriveChat accesses and how it is used.

What DriveChat accesses

  • Your basic Google account info (name, email address and profile photo). These are used to show your profile to the people you chat with.
  • Google Drive, limited to files DriveChat creates (the drive.file permission). DriveChat cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.

Where your data is stored

  • Everything DriveChat saves goes into a folder named DriveChat in your own Google Drive. That includes your messages, attachments, contact list and settings.
  • Messages, attachments, voice notes, read receipts and typing indicators are end-to-end encrypted in your browser before they are saved. Only the people in a conversation can decrypt them. The app's developer cannot read them, and neither can Google.
  • Your encryption key is protected by a backup password that only you know. It is never sent to the developer.
  • Your profile (display name, email, photo and public encryption key) and the encrypted message files are readable by anyone who has your personal invite link. This is how your contacts receive your messages. Without the encryption keys, the message contents can't be read.
  • Voice and video calls connect directly between the two browsers and are encrypted. Call audio and video are not recorded or stored.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not operate servers that receive, store or process your messages or Google data.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling or training AI models.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party trackers. DriveChat uses your browser's local storage only for settings and for keeping your encryption key on your device.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DriveChat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, delete the DriveChat folder from your Google Drive and empty the trash. You can also remove DriveChat's access at any time in your Google Account permissions. Messages you sent stay in your Drive until you delete them, and messages others sent you stay in their Drives.

Children

DriveChat is not intended for children under 13.
